## Key Insights

- Sales peak during late-night and early-morning hours, with the highest activity occurring around midnight.
- Depop listings sell faster on average than Mercari and Poshmark, indicating stronger buyer velocity.
- Depop supports higher sales volume at lower prices, while Mercari facilitates higher-priced but less frequent transactions.

## Methodology Notes

- Time-of-day analysis was limited to transactions with available timestamps (primarily Depop).
- Days-to-sell values were filtered to a realistic range (0–365 days) to account for relisted and legacy inventory.
- Poshmark results were included but de-emphasized due to limited sample size.
